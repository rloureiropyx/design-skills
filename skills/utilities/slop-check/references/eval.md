# Eval

Read this at edit step 4 and run it against your own edit, before returning anything.

**You run it.** Do not spawn a reviewer agent, and do not ask the user to check. A separate reviewer
re-reads the draft without the voice-signal note you wrote at step 2, which is exactly the context
that decides most of these.

Any failure: fix it, then run the list again from the top. A fix can break a check that passed.

---

## Voice

- [ ] The three-to-five voice signals from step 2 are still present in the edited text.
- [ ] Every pattern in the profile's `## Slop-rule overrides` section survived untouched.
- [ ] No sentence was changed only because you would have written it differently.
- [ ] The draft's unevenness survived — paragraphs are not all the same length, sections are not all
      equally polished.
- [ ] Humour, profanity, asides, and digressions are intact.
- [ ] Hedges carrying real uncertainty are intact; only throat-clearing hedges were cut.

## Patterns

- [ ] No throat-clearing opener, faux-insight setup, or rhetorical setup remains.
- [ ] No binary contrast or negative listing remains where the discarded half carried nothing.
- [ ] No colon reveal, no `-ing` significance clause, no importance puffery.
- [ ] No unnamed attribution — "experts agree", "studies show" — survived without a source.
- [ ] No fake-strong verb where `is`, `has`, or `does` would carry it.
- [ ] One name per thing. Nothing is called three names in four sentences.
- [ ] The piece ends on a real sentence: no fake-profound kicker, no recap.
- [ ] Sentence shapes vary. No stack of three fragments, no paragraph of identical constructions.
- [ ] Em-dash density is inside the profile's tolerance, or inside one-or-two per longer passage when
      there is no profile.
- [ ] Formatting is earned: no emoji headings, no mid-sentence bold for emphasis, no bullets where
      prose reads better, no heading over two sentences.

## Discipline

- [ ] Nothing was invented — no claim, number, name, example, or causal transition absent from the
      original.
- [ ] Nothing was restructured. Sections are in the same order and no paragraph was cut for being
      unnecessary.
- [ ] No slop was replaced with better slop. Kickers and rhetorical setups were deleted, not upgraded.
- [ ] The meaning is unchanged. Nothing now says more, or less, than the writer claimed.
- [ ] Anything you noticed but deliberately left is named in **What changed**.

## Final read

- [ ] Would the writer recognise this as their own?
- [ ] Read cold, does it sound like a person rather than like clean prose?
- [ ] Is every change either a named pattern, an empty phrase, a repetition, or a sentence that could
      not be followed?
- [ ] No word from the `Words to cut` list and no phrase from the empty-phrase list survived, unless
      the profile protects it.
- [ ] Is **What changed** short, specific, and honest about what you left alone?
- [ ] Was this checked directly against this file, by you, without a second agent?

## Detect mode only

- [ ] Every finding names the pattern, quotes the line, and gives the fix in a few words.
- [ ] Nothing was rewritten.
- [ ] No score, rating, or percentage.
- [ ] No claim about who or what wrote the text.
- [ ] Nothing listed that the profile's overrides section protects.
- [ ] No weak findings padding the list. If there were none, that is what was reported.
