# Evidence inputs

Read this at step 5, once an assumption has passed all four gate questions.

Ask the designer one evidence-input question at a time, and recommend the exact query or filter to use. "More research" is not an evidence request.

## Metrics input

Specify:

- assumption and decision it informs;
- behavior or event to measure;
- population, segment, or cohort;
- timeframe and comparison baseline;
- funnel step, frequency, or outcome when relevant;
- result that would support, weaken, or split the frame.

## Research-library input

Specify:

- assumption and decision it informs;
- user or segment;
- situation, task, or moment to search for;
- observations, workarounds, objections, or consequences needed;
- relevant recency or product context;
- finding that would support, weaken, or split the frame.

## Focused evidence request

Use this only when neither source can answer the uncertainty:

- assumption to test;
- decision it informs;
- current evidence and provenance;
- smallest credible method;
- signal that supports or weakens the assumption;
- stopping condition;
- owner, when known.

## After evidence arrives

Return to the baseline story and the candidate frames. New evidence can revive a rejected frame — that is what the rejection reasons are recorded for.

General research that cannot change a decision does not block framing. Say so, record the assumption as accepted risk, and move on.
