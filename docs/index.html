Work out the problem before you design the solution.

Ten skills for one short recipe: work out what the problem actually is, try two or three directions and pick one, then write it up so people can decide. The rest draw, diagram or publish the result once something has been decided — in your voice rather than a model's.

Process

Skills that end in a decision rather than a deliverable.

  • Process · 01

    Frame before you design

    problem-framing

    Turns a request into a written problem with the evidence it rests on: quotes kept apart from interpretations, more than one explanation considered, and what was set aside written down. Has a quick mode — five questions, one page — for small solo work.

    • Explainer
    • Scene script
    • SKILL.md

    Buys time to explore solutions. Does not buy permission to build.

  • Process · 02

    Pick a direction

    shaping

    Two people can hold two defensible solutions with no shared test between them. This writes the test down: numbered requirements, two or three lettered directions, a fit check with only two possible answers, and unknowns flagged so they cannot be waved through. Directions improve across versions, and discarded ones stay visible with the reason.

    • Explainer
    • Scene script
    • SKILL.md

    Stops at one selected direction with no unresolved ⚠️ flags.

Artifacts

Skills that produce the thing people react to.

  • Artifacts · 01

    Breadboarding

    breadboarding

    Sketches a solution in words instead of pixels: the places a person can be, what they can do or see in each one, and which action takes them where. Too coarse to express layout, which is what lets people argue about the mechanism first. Shaping uses it to make each direction concrete.

    • Explainer
    • SKILL.md

    No article yet — the explainer is the write-up.

  • Artifacts · 02

    Wireframes

    wireframes

    Decides what a screen must do, what goes on it, and how priority reads, then hands that to a renderer. Scale, fidelity, annotation weight and abstraction are four separate axes, so "more detail" adds content, states and rules before it adds polish. Colour means something or it is not there, and one screen shows one real moment.

    • Explainer
    • SKILL.md

    No article yet — the explainer is the write-up.

  • Artifacts · 03

    The drawing is the easy part

    diagrams

    Decides what a diagram should show and why before anything is drawn: intention, audience, scope, scale. The type is chosen from what the reader is stuck on — time, arrangement or context — not from the word they used. Twelve recipes, a stopping rule, and a key that is never allowed to become a decoder ring.

    • Explainer
    • Scene script
    • SKILL.md

    A diagram fails before anything is drawn — nine scenes on the four decisions that come first.

  • Artifacts · 04

    One read, one decision

    proposal

    The document people read once and then decide on: the problem, the time budget, the chosen direction, the rabbit holes, what is deliberately out, and what is still uncertain. Everything carried from the two documents before it, and it refuses to be written while an unknown is still flagged.

    • Explainer
    • Scene script
    • SKILL.md

    Decidable in one read, or it is not finished.

Renderers

Skills that materialise a decision made elsewhere. Give one an undecided idea and it will produce a tidy picture of confusion.

  • Renderers · 01

    Scroll is not a story

    scrollytelling

    Long-form web articles whose diagrams change as you scroll. Goal and tone get chosen from a short menu first, then a scene-by-scene script, then approval, then the build — and the result is checked across a device matrix rather than admired on a laptop.

  • Renderers · 02

    Draw what was decided

    tldraw-skill

    Draws flows, journey maps, service blueprints, concept models, sitemaps, breadboards and wireframes on a local canvas. It owns coordinates, connectors, contrast and legibility — never the content. Needs the tldraw offline app running on the same machine.

Utilities

Skills the others call while they draft. They own neither a decision nor an artifact, and they share one document between them.

  • Utilities · 01

    Writing Style

    writing-style

    Reads two or three things you have already written and turns them into a profile every drafting skill loads first: voice in a paragraph, a few signature moves with the line quoted underneath each, defaults you can check a draft against, and the tics an editing pass must leave alone. It asks for writing rather than adjectives, because people describe the voice they want, not the one they have.

    • Explainer
    • SKILL.md

    Set up once, in about five minutes. Saved outside the plugin so an update cannot delete it.

  • Utilities · 02

    Slop Check

    slop-check

    Removes seventeen named patterns — throat-clearing openers, colon reveals, importance puffery, fake-profound endings — and nothing else. Asked what is wrong instead of asked for a fix, it names each pattern and quotes the line. It will not score writing and will not guess whether AI wrote it: detectors guess, a quoted line is evidence.

    • Explainer
    • SKILL.md

    A tic your profile protects is not a finding. That precedence is the whole point.
